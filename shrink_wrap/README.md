# shrink_wrap

The wire format: `BufReader` and `BufWriter`, the `SerializeShrinkWrap` / `DeserializeShrinkWrap` traits (and their
owned forms), the bit-sized numbers and varints, the special types (`RefVec`, `TailBytes`, `OptionAnyVec`, ...) and
`Series`. `no_std`, no allocator needed. See the [repository README](../README.md) and the pages in
[`docs/`](../docs).
